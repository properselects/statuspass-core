How it works

Three steps. Two of them are one link.

STEP 01

Issue a pass

Type who it's for. That's the whole form. Send your client one link — they add their logo, pick a color, and tap “Add to Wallet” on the same page.

STEP 02

Work like you already do

Move a Trello card, transition a Jira issue, or drag on StatusPass's own board. The update writes itself in client-safe language — no names leaked, no deadlines invented.

STEP 03

Their phone buzzes

The pass refreshes on every device it lives on. On iPhone, your sentence is the lock-screen banner. The QR opens the demo shelf — every Loom, preview, and deliverable, newest first.
